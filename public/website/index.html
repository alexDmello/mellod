Compliance & Credentials

Fully Registered & Certified

Mellod Biofuels operates under all required government registrations, giving your food business complete peace of mind.

🏛️

FSSAI RUCO Registered

Mellod Biofuels is a registered aggregator under FSSAI's Repurpose Used Cooking Oil (RUCO) initiative — the national standard for UCO collection in India.

📄

Full Documentation Chain

Every collection is logged with tamper-proof digital receipts, agent ID, GPS coordinates, and litres collected — a complete paper trail for your compliance records.

🔏

DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

Your business and personal data is handled in full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We never share your data with third parties.

Certified Feedstock Supplier

Our collected UCO is supplied as certified, traceable biodiesel feedstock — supporting India's national biofuel mission and your business's sustainability goals.
