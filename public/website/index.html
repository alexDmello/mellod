Frequently Asked Questions

Everything About UCO Collection in Bangalore

Clear answers regarding Used Cooking Oil regulations, FSSAI RUCO compliance, and doorstep pickup services in Bengaluru.

Mellod Biofuels is an authorized Used Cooking Oil (UCO) aggregator operating in Bangalore in partnership with FSSAI RUCO-registered processing units. We collect waste cooking oil directly from commercial kitchens and redirect it safely into certified biodiesel manufacturing.
We offer the highest competitive market rates per litre for Used Cooking Oil in Bangalore based on oil purity and quality. Payouts can be settled instantly via bank transfer, UPI, or cash upon collection.
Under FSSAI guidelines, food business operators (FBOs) using more than 50 litres of cooking oil per day are mandated to dispose of UCO via authorized aggregators. Every pickup by Mellod Biofuels generates an official digital RUCO receipt and monthly compliance certificate for your audit records.
Yes, doorstep collection is 100% free across Bangalore. Our field pickup team visits your kitchen, measures the volume, issues a tamper-proof digital receipt, and safely transports the oil.
We cover all major Bangalore dining and commercial zones including Indiranagar, Koramangala, Whitefield, HSR Layout, Electronic City, Jayanagar, MG Road, Marathahalli, Bellandur, Yelahanka, Hebbal, Malleshwaram, and Rajajinagar.
